Does this really work?

Yes. Here is why.

🔬

Light weight still builds muscle. Studies comparing light and heavy loads found that sets taken close to failure build similar muscle. Bodyweight, bands and slow reps qualify.

📈

Beginners grow fastest. The first year of training is when the body responds most. Starting now is perfect timing.

🍑

Every glute muscle gets hit. Bridges, squats, hinges and lunges for the big glute; kicks and raises for the upper-side curve.

🪜

It gets harder on purpose. Four seasons step up from bodyweight to tempo, single-leg, bands and dumbbells, so the glutes always have a reason to grow.

⌛

The waist is built from both ends. Fat loss and deep-core work tighten the middle while glutes and shoulders widen the frame around it.
